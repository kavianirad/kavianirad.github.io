Short bio

Hossein Kavianirad is a doctoral researcher at the Chair of Information-oriented Control (ITR), Technical University of Munich, advised by Prof. Sandra Hirche. His research covers control theory, learning-based and human-centric control, and shared and cooperative control of hybrid exoskeletons and wearable robots. He develops controllers for input-redundant systems that distribute effort between an exoskeleton and functional electrical stimulation while accounting for the user's intent, using dynamic allocation with stability guarantees, model predictive control and EMG-based intent estimation, tested with healthy participants and stroke patients in the EU Horizon 2020 project ReHyb. He holds an M.Sc. in Mechanical Engineering from Sharif University of Technology and a B.Sc. from Shiraz University, and received the IEEE-EMBS IECBES 2022 Best Paper Award.

Research experience

  • 04/2021 – present

    Doctoral Researcher

    Chair of Information-oriented Control (ITR), Technical University of Munich
    • Designed shared and cooperative control for hybrid FES-exoskeletons: impedance-based shared control, MPC-based FES control, and a real-time dynamic allocator that redistributes torque between FES and exoskeleton under heterogeneous actuator constraints, with a stability proof. Extended it to personalized allocation based on participant-specific attainable sets.
    • Identified user-specific nonlinear, delayed and time-varying neuromuscular models from sparse data with learning-based methods, and estimated volitional torque from EMG with a novel adaptive filter (Best Paper Award).
    • Ran studies with healthy participants and stroke patients on the NESM-γ elbow exoskeleton (Scuola Superiore Sant'Anna), the HRX-1 wrist robot (HumanRobotiX) and FES/EMG hardware (Tecnalia) in the EU Horizon 2020 project ReHyb (GA 871767), with Imperial College London and Schön Klinik Bad Aibling.
