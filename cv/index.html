Short bio

Hossein Kavianirad is a doctoral researcher at the Chair of Information-oriented Control (ITR), Technical University of Munich, advised by Prof. Sandra Hirche. His research develops cooperative shared control for hybrid FES-exoskeletons that combine functional electrical stimulation with robotic actuation to restore arm and hand function after stroke, within the EU Horizon project ReHyb. He holds an M.Sc. in Mechanical Engineering from Sharif University of Technology and a B.Sc. from Shiraz University. He received the IEEE-EMBS IECBES 2022 Best Paper Award.
